## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out Meridian Financial Group's (FFG) commitment to information security and establishes the framework for setting information security objectives, in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 5.2.

## 2. Policy Statement

Top management is committed to:

1. Protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of FFG's information assets and those of its customers, in line with business, legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements.
2. Establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving the ISMS in accordance with ISO/IEC 27001:2022.
3. Setting measurable information security objectives (see `Information-Security-Objectives.md`) and reviewing them at planned intervals.
4. Ensuring the availability of resources needed for the ISMS.
5. Communicating the importance of effective information security management and of conforming to ISMS requirements to all personnel and relevant interested parties.
6. Ensuring the ISMS achieves its intended outcomes.
7. Directing and supporting persons to contribute to the effectiveness of the ISMS.
8. Promoting continual improvement.
9. Supporting other relevant management roles to demonstrate leadership as it applies to their areas of responsibility.
10. Applying a risk-based approach to information security decision-making, consistent with the Risk Assessment and Risk Treatment Methodology (`02-Risk-Management`).

## 3. Scope of Applicability

This policy applies to all FFG employees, contractors, third parties, and systems within the ISMS scope defined in the ISMS Scope Statement (`ISMS-Scope-Statement.md`). It supersedes any prior information security policy.

## 5. Roles and Responsibilities

Detailed in `Roles-Responsibilities-Authorities.md`. In summary:

- **Top Management (CEO)**: ultimate accountability for the ISMS
- **CISO**: day-to-day ownership, risk reporting, policy maintenance
- **Information Security Team**: implementation and operation of controls
- **All Personnel**: compliance with this policy and reporting of suspected incidents

## 6. Compliance and Consequences

Non-compliance with this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or contract, in accordance with FFG's HR disciplinary process, and may result in legal action where unlawful activity is involved.

## 7. Review

This policy is reviewed at least annually by the CISO and approved by top management, or earlier following a significant security incident, regulatory change, or organizational change.
