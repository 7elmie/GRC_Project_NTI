## Part A — Procedure

### 1. Purpose

Ensures top management reviews the ISMS at planned intervals for continuing suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness, per Clause 9.3.

### 2. Frequency

Formal Management Review is conducted **at least annually**. Interim reviews may occur at quarterly ISMS Steering Committee meetings but do not replace the formal annual review.

### 3. Required Inputs (Clause 9.3.2)

Per ISO 27001:2022, the review must consider:

1. Status of actions from previous management reviews
2. Changes in external and internal issues relevant to the ISMS
3. Changes in needs and expectations of interested parties relevant to the ISMS
4. Feedback on ISMS performance, including trends in:
   - Nonconformities and corrective actions
   - Monitoring and measurement results (Clause 9.1)
   - Audit results (internal and external)
   - Fulfillment of information security objectives
5. Feedback from interested parties
6. Results of risk assessment and status of the risk treatment plan
7. Opportunities for continual improvement

### 4. Required Outputs (Clause 9.3.3)

Decisions related to continual improvement opportunities and any need for changes to the ISMS, including:

- Changes to the Information Security Policy or objectives
- Resource allocation decisions
- Risk acceptance decisions
- Assignment of new action items with owners and due dates

### 5. Process

1. CISO compiles the review pack (inputs above) and circulates 5 business days prior.
2. Review meeting held with top management and relevant role owners.
3. Minutes recorded using the template in Part B.
4. Action items logged in the ISMS Action Tracker with owner and due date.
5. Minutes and action tracker retained as documented information (Clause 9.3.3, final paragraph).
