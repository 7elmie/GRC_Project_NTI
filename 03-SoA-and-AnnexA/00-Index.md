# 03 — Statement of Applicability & Annex A: Index

## Purpose

This folder contains the Statement of Applicability (SoA), required by ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 6.1.3(d), covering all 93 controls of Annex A (2022 revision), organized by theme: Organizational (A.5, 37 controls), People (A.6, 8 controls), Physical (A.7, 14 controls), Technological (A.8, 34 controls).

## Documents

| # | Document | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Statement of Applicability | Applicability decision, justification, implementation status, and evidence reference for every Annex A control |

## Methodology

Per Clause 6.1.3, the SoA must contain:

- (b) Justification for inclusion of necessary controls
- (c) Whether necessary controls are implemented or not
- (d) Justification for excluding any Annex A control

Necessary controls are derived from: the Risk Treatment Plan (`02-Risk-Management/Risk-Treatment-Plan.md`), legal/regulatory/contractual requirements, and business requirements. **All 93 Annex A:2022 controls are determined applicable to FFG** given the scale and nature of its digital banking/payments operations; no control is excluded on the basis of "not relevant" — where a control is currently only partially implemented, this is disclosed transparently as an open item tracked in the Risk Treatment Plan or Internal Audit findings, not treated as inapplicable.

## Traceability

Each control below references, where relevant:
- The Risk Register ID(s) it mitigates (`02-Risk-Management/Risk-Register.md`)
- The Policy/Procedure that implements it (`05-Policies-and-Procedures`)
- Its current implementation status, which internal audit verifies (`04-Internal-Audit-Execution`)
