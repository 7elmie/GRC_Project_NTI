# 04 — Internal Audit Execution: Index

## Purpose

This folder documents the internal audit function's execution against ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 9.2 (Internal Audit) and Clause 10.1/10.2 (Nonconformity and Corrective Action, Continual Improvement).

## Scenario for This Folder

Per the project brief: **FFG already has an ISMS and claims ISO 27001:2022 compliance.** This folder performs the internal audit that tests that claim — including deliberately surfacing the gaps already flagged as "Partially Implemented" in the Statement of Applicability (`03-SoA-and-AnnexA/Statement-of-Applicability.md`), consistent with a realistic first-cycle internal audit of a genuinely operating ISMS (not a "perfect ISMS" audit, which would not be credible).

## Documents

| # | Document | Clause | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Internal Audit Programme | 9.2.1 | Multi-cycle programme: scope, frequency, methods, criteria, independence |
| 2 | Internal Audit Plan and Checklist | 9.2.2 | This cycle's specific audit plan, scope, and clause-by-clause / control-by-control checklist |
| 3 | Internal Audit Findings Report | 9.2.2, 10.1, 10.2 | Nonconformities, observations, opportunities for improvement, root cause, corrective actions |

## Independence

Per `01-ISMS-Mandatory-Documents/Roles-Responsibilities-Authorities.md` §5, the Internal Audit function reports independently to the CEO/Audit Committee, not to the CISO, to satisfy Clause 9.2.2(c) objectivity and impartiality requirements — the auditor did not audit their own area of responsibility.
