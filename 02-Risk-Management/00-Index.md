# 02 — Risk Management: Index

## Purpose

This folder contains the risk assessment and risk treatment documented information required by ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 6.1.2 and 6.1.3, and Clause 8.2/8.3 (operational risk assessment/treatment).

## Documents

| # | Document | Clause | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Risk Assessment and Risk Treatment Methodology | 6.1.2, 6.1.3 | Defines risk criteria, process, and treatment options |
| 2 | Risk Register | 6.1.2, 8.2 | Identified risks, assessed likelihood/impact, current risk level |
| 3 | Risk Treatment Plan | 6.1.3(e), 8.3 | Treatment decisions, actions, owners, timelines, residual risk |

## Traceability

- Every risk in the Risk Register that is treated by "Mitigate" must map to ≥1 Annex A control in `03-SoA-and-AnnexA/Statement-of-Applicability.md`.
- Risks tied to an Information Security Objective are cross-referenced in `01-ISMS-Mandatory-Documents/Information-Security-Objectives.md`.
- Risk acceptances above the defined threshold require CEO/top management sign-off per `01-ISMS-Mandatory-Documents/Roles-Responsibilities-Authorities.md`.
