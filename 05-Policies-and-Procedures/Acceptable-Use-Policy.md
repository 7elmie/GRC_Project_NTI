## 1. Purpose

Defines acceptable use of FFG's information, IT systems, and associated assets by employees, contractors, and third parties.

## 2. General Use

- FFG systems and accounts are provided for business purposes; incidental personal use is permitted if it does not interfere with duties, consume excessive resources, or violate this policy or law.
- Users must not attempt to bypass security controls, install unauthorized software, or connect unauthorized devices to the corporate network.
- Intellectual property created using FFG resources belongs to FFG (A.5.32), except where otherwise contractually agreed.

## 3. §Remote Work

- Remote access requires MFA and an approved, managed device (corporate laptop or approved BYOD enrolled in MDM).
- Public/untrusted Wi-Fi use requires the corporate VPN or Zero Trust access client.
- Sensitive information must not be printed, photographed, or left visible in non-secure remote locations.

## 4. §Clear Desk and Clear Screen

- Workstations must be locked when unattended (automatic lock ≤ 5 minutes of inactivity).
- Sensitive physical documents (e.g., printed customer data) must not be left unattended on desks and must be securely stored or shredded when no longer needed.

## 5. Equipment Off-Premises

- Company equipment taken off-premises (laptops, mobile devices) must remain within the user's control, be encrypted, and be reported immediately if lost or stolen (A.7.9).

## 6. Prohibited Activities

- Sharing credentials; disabling security agents (EDR, MDM); using FFG systems to harass, discriminate, or engage in unlawful activity; unauthorized data exfiltration (e.g., to personal cloud storage or personal email).

## 7. Monitoring

FFG reserves the right to monitor use of its systems for security and compliance purposes, consistent with applicable law and the Data Classification and Handling Policy's treatment of employee data as PII.

## 8. Enforcement

Violations are handled per the Disciplinary Process (Information Security Policy §6).
