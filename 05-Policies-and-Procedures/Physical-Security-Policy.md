## 1. Purpose

Protects FFG premises, equipment, and personnel from unauthorized physical access, damage, and environmental hazards, covering both directly managed facilities (head office) and third-party managed facilities (data center — see RISK-010).

## 2. Perimeter and Entry Control (A.7.1, A.7.2)

- Head office: badge access at all external entry points; visitor sign-in with escort required at all times.
- Data center: managed by the co-location provider under its own perimeter/entry controls; FFG relies on and annually reviews the provider's SOC 2 Type II report as evidence, per the Supplier Relationships Policy.

## 3. Secure Areas (A.7.3, A.7.6)

- Server rooms, network closets, and areas processing Restricted data are designated secure areas with restricted badge-list access, logged entry, and no unescorted visitor access.
- Work in secure areas is limited to authorized personnel; unattended access by cleaning/maintenance staff requires supervision or independent verification logging.

## 4. Monitoring (A.7.4)

- CCTV coverage at all entry/exit points and secure areas, with retention per the Records Retention schedule.
- Data center physical monitoring is the provider's responsibility, evidenced via their compliance reporting.

## 5. Environmental Protection (A.7.5, A.7.8)

- Server rooms/data center: fire suppression, temperature/humidity monitoring, and power redundancy (UPS + generator) per provider SLA.
- Equipment sited to minimize environmental and unauthorized-access risk (not in publicly accessible areas).

## 6. Deliveries and Loading Areas

Delivery/loading areas are physically separated from areas processing Restricted data where feasible, with incoming equipment inspected before connection to the network.

## 7. Third-Party Facility Reliance

Where a control (e.g., 7.1–7.5, 7.11, 7.12) is delivered via a third-party facility rather than direct FFG operation, this policy is satisfied via the Supplier Relationships Policy's due diligence and annual reassessment process, not by FFG operating the control directly.
